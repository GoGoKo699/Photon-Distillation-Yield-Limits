# Scientific account

How many independent imperfect photons must a passive optical apparatus consume to herald one photon with a much smaller first-order internal-mode error?

Start with [the model and claim hierarchy](MODEL_AND_CLAIMS.md), then follow [the stable yield and photon-cost theorem](THEOREM.md). The [uniform proof details](PROOF_DETAILS.md) control arbitrary survivor couplings and vacuum extensions. [Fourier constructions and sharp small batches](OPTICAL_CONSTRUCTION.md) supply attainment and the two-, three-, and four-photon optima.

The [output-number mechanism](PHYSICAL_MECHANISM.md) derives the necessary thermal number marginal of near-optimal apparatuses. [Physical interpretation](PHYSICAL_INTERPRETATION.md) connects the conditional internal state to two-copy interference and explains the apparatus hypotheses. Number statistics before heralding and internal purity after heralding are distinct observables.

The derivative at zero input error is taken for each fixed apparatus before increasing its size. The central bound concerns ideal mean input-photon consumption with a predetermined survivor; its first-order qualification and resource assumptions remain part of the theorem.

For specialist checking, use the [proof map](PROOF_MAP.md), [attribution](../references/ATTRIBUTION.md), [structured bibliography](../references/SOURCES.json), and [verification guide](../verification/README.md).
