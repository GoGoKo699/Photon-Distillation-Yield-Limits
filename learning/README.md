# Learn photon-distillation yield limits

Use the [30-milestone map](MILESTONES.md) as the common route through the project,
and the [Tichy bridge](TICHY_BRIDGE.md) for the optical foundations and a complete
two-photon example. The single educational anchor remains Malte C. Tichy's
*Interference of Identical Particles from Entanglement to Boson-Sampling*
(J. Phys. B 47, 103001, 2014; arXiv:1312.4266v2).

The map has ten background/preliminaries milestones and twenty
narrative/innovation milestones. The first phase assembles a heralded optical
experiment from states, interference and conditioning. The second builds the
resource theorem from accepted amplitudes and then connects it to attaining
circuits, necessary output statistics and interference visibility.

The milestones describe understanding targets for any reader, including an LLM.
They contain no learner-specific assessment or progress. Use the selected source
sections in the bridge, rather than treating the whole review as a prerequisite.
The general converse and its uniform estimates are supplied by the project's
[scientific account](../science/README.md), not by the educational anchor.

Specialists can go directly to the [model](../science/MODEL_AND_CLAIMS.md),
[theorem](../science/THEOREM.md), [proof map](../science/PROOF_MAP.md), and
[verification](../verification/README.md).
